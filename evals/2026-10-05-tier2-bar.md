# A tier 2 bar for the broad selections, October 5

Status: wording chosen, not yet in Notion. Applying it re-screens about 13,000 papers in one
run (see Rollout).

## Problem

Tier 2 had no bar beyond being on the subject: the prompt defines it as "central to the
subject", and the shared rubric says incremental work is "at most tier 2" and "when in doubt,
tier 2". On October 5 (run 42), 213 of 742 papers were tier 2. Verdicts since September 28:

| selection | tier 2 | share of all papers | share of its members |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| agents | 1054 | 15.0% | 55% |
| efficiency-and-architecture | 587 | 8.4% | 26% |
| reasoning-and-rl | 528 | 7.5% | 28% |
| alignment-and-safety | 490 | 7.0% | 24% |
| interpretability | 161 | 2.3% | 20% |

## What was tested

The October 5 feed replayed through the full cascade (Luna screen, GLM 5.3 high rescreen,
Muse confirm) with `--spec`-style YAML specs and no database writes. Only the rubric of the
four broad selections changed; subjects, keywords, the prompt, and the other three selections
did not. The comparison is against the production verdicts for the same papers, so it mixes
the rubric's effect with run-to-run variation. The unchanged selections measure that
variation: interpretability had 28 papers cross the tier 3/4 line and 3 cross the 2/3 line.
There are no labels for these selections, so recall is not measured.

Two wordings, both replacing the rubric's last two sentences:

- strict: tier 2 needs "a new method with evidence on more than one benchmark or domain", and
  "small-scale or single-setting studies" are tier 3.
- soft (`specs/2026-10-05-tier2-bar/`): "a new method with real evidence that it works", and
  the single-setting clause is dropped.

## Result

709 papers completed in both replays (23 and 13 calls failed with a provider 404).

| | best tier 1 or 2 | agents t2 | efficiency t2 | reasoning-and-rl t2 | alignment t2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| production | 192 | 89 | 46 | 41 | 48 |
| strict | 126 | 51 | 25 | 22 | 37 |
| soft | 147 | 66 | 29 | 25 | 39 |

Of the 19 tier 2 papers the general digest featured that day, strict kept 14 of 17 that
replayed and soft kept 18 of 19. Strict dropped analysis papers whose abstract names one
benchmark (looped-transformer shared memory, RLVR enrichment, trajectory-judge); soft keeps
them and still drops single-domain applications and position pieces (ColPackAgent, Coco, the
agent-protocol threat model). Under soft, one production tier 1 (2602.09009) came out tier 2
with the tier 1 wording unchanged, and 2610.02835 came out tier 1 in both replays.

Decision: soft. Agents is still the largest at 66; narrowing its subject is the next lever.
Cost: $0.60 per replay, plus a 62-paper pilot ($0.05) and one interrupted run.

## Rollout

Editing the four rubrics selects 13,147 papers for the next run (members of any age plus
30-day keyword hits; union across the four). At run 42's rate that is about 5.5 hours of
screening and roughly $12 to $15, and it meets two limits: the daily workflow fails a run
unfinished after 6 hours, and the projection reads at most 3000 changed papers per run.

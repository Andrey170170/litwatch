## What was tested

Whether v1's classification and grading keep the papers each cohort holds positive on the two CoT Focus topics, per
stage (feedback.md, evaluation): the classifier alone, membership by any route, grading on its own (every labelled
paper graded whatever classification said), and end to end. Cohorts are never pooled (`evals/labels/README.md`):
**owner** is the owner's word (7 positives); **provisional** and **legacy** are 0.14's tier 1–2 papers on the CoT
selections, model output the owner has not confirmed, so recall there means "keeps what 0.14 kept"; **silver** is the
first v1's two-lab grading. No miss is reported yet, so there is no `miss` cohort.

**The sample.** The full set (1,455 labels on 1,049 papers, 1,361 paper-topic gradings) was estimated at $9.59 from
this run's per-call costs (classification $0.00026 a paper, a Focus grading $0.0069 a pair, nearly all of it the
judge), over the $5 allowed without asking. So: the owner cohort in full, every positive of every cohort (positives
are what every recall number is over, so these numbers are the full run's), and a sample of the other cohorts'
negatives with seed 20261014 (`--negatives legacy=60 --negatives silver=40`; provisional has none). 535 labels on
325 papers, 445 gradings. Spend: $2.90, plus $0.26 for a 20-paper trial whose answers the run reused. No call failed.

## Findings

- **End to end, v1 keeps what the first v1 kept, but for two monitoring papers.** The owner's 7 positives all come
  through (5 of 5, 2 of 2), as they did for the first v1 once its labels were corrected. On 0.14's positives
  (provisional): CoT faithfulness 38 of 39 (first v1, `evals/2026-10-09-v1-watch.md`: 37 of 39), CoT monitoring 48 of
  51 (first v1: 50 of 51, its one loss being 2609.15517, lost here too). The first v1 counted a grade of relevant or
  above, this run a Fit of related or above, so the scales are close but not the same. Every loss is at grading,
  none at classification.
- **Membership recall is 1.00 in every cohort, and the uncertain band does the work.** The classifier alone keeps
  22 of 39 faithfulness and 39 of 51 monitoring positives on provisional; the band [0.1, 0.5) brings in every one it
  missed. The keyword check never rescued a positive the band had not: it is insurance here, not a route.
- **Every positive lost is graded adjacent by both labs**, so no split hides it: 2608.29956 (faithfulness),
  2608.28623, 2609.15517, 2609.22119 (monitoring), all 0.14 tier 1–2, and 2605.09502 (silver, monitoring).
  2609.15517 is the paper the first v1 lost too, and both graders called it adjacent then. These are the losses V5
  asks to be looked at: whether the rubrics or 0.14's tiers are right on them is the owner's call.
- **The owner read the five losses (2026-10-15) and sided with the graders on four.** 2608.29956 is adjacent to
  faithfulness (latent reasoning inferred without a trace, one model family, GSM8K only); 2609.15517 is adjacent to
  monitoring (the training side, DPO on reasoning segments); 2609.22119 is adjacent (its subject is evaluation
  awareness itself, the trace is not the instrument); 2605.09502 is adjacent to monitoring and closer to
  faithfulness, where it is left unlabelled. 2608.28623 is a weak related on monitoring, closer to sycophancy (a
  benchmark of sycophancy inside reasoning chains that builds no monitor): the one real loss, a borderline case
  that asks for no rubric change. 0.14's tier 2 and the silver tier were too generous on the other four. The five
  are owner labels in `fit.csv` since (origin `v5_review`); this report keeps the labels it ran on, so on today's
  labels the owner cohort's monitoring recall would read 2 of 3, with 2608.28623 the miss.
- **Few negatives come through.** Of the owner's 205 negative pairs, 1 does: 2609.30768 ("Does Thinking Help
  Fairness?", labelled none on monitoring), a member by the band and graded related (judge adjacent, check related;
  the more inclusive stands). Of the sampled legacy tier-3 pairs, 5 of 60 come through; of silver's negatives, 1 of 40.
- **The labs split often on monitoring**: 20 of the 51 provisional monitoring positives, 6 of the 39 faithfulness ones.

Shell fixed from round 1: desktop B (sidebar), phone C (drawer). This round asks what it should feel like. Switch directions at the top, and Light/Dark for each; both frames are live. On a phone this page shows only the phone, full screen, so the swipe can be tried for real.

The drawer. Put a finger (or the mouse) in the left edge strip and drag right: the drawer follows and settles open or shut by where you let go and how fast. Drag it left, or tap the shade, to close. A drag that starts vertical scrolls instead and never opens it; a rightward drag outside the edge does nothing. "Edge" sets the strip's width, "zone" shows it.

Questions

  1. Which direction: A, B, C or D? Or a mix, such as Night City's colours with Mission control's type?
  2. How far should it go: everything shown (scanlines, glitch, cut corners, drafting grid) or only the colours and type?
  3. Light and dark: equally good, or is one of them the real app and the other a courtesy?
  4. The swipe: does it feel right, and what edge width works on your phone? Phones keep the very edge for their own back gesture, so the strip has to start a little inward.
  5. On a paper or topic page the bar shows ‹ instead of ☰. Should an edge swipe there go back, the iOS way, and the drawer open only from top-level screens?

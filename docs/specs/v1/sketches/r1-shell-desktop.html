Coarse structure only: where navigation lives and how much of the screen each part gets. Colours and type are placeholders. Every frame is live: click tabs, topics, digests, papers, "History".

Today's shape. Three tabs in a header, one centred reading column. Simple, but wide screens get empty margins and every move is a page change.

B · Sidebar with topics

Like T3 Code's project list: the tabs and every followed topic always visible on the left, each with its icon and a count of what's new. Topics are one click from anywhere and always in the same place.

C · Three panes

Mail-client layout: sidebar, a list, and the opened item beside it. Going through a list never leaves it: History days on the left open the day on the right; a paper opens beside the table.

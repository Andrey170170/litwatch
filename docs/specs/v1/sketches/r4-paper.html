The paper page, the last rung of every list. It reads top to bottom as the ladder: the Gist, the Brief (or the abstract where there is none), the Deep card, then the paper itself (links, versions, where it was published). Above the ladder: the paper's level and topics, its title and facts, Save and the links. Each topic the paper is in gets a row with its grade there, the verdict behind it, thumbs with their reasons, and a label. A keeps everything in one column, all open; B puts the actions, topics and facts in a rail beside the ladder when there is room; C is A with the topic rows and the Deep card's sections folded to one line each. Open papers in different states:

Things to try: a 👍 or 👎 on a topic row asks for the reason, and the note at the bottom says what it did. Label sets your Fit for that topic. ✓ and ✗ beside the Gist and Brief say whether the text is accurate (faint until hovered). Prior work and Among the collection open those papers' pages; a prior paper outside the collection opens the archive's page with Process and Deep read. On the phone, Save and the thumbs sit in the bottom bar, and a swipe across the page (away from the edge) steps to the previous or next paper of the list you came from; the number in the bar is its place there.

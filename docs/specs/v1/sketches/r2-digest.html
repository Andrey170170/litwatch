Night City, toned down: no scanlines, plainer numbers. The ladder is fixed by the spec: History → a day → a digest → a paper. This round asks how you move along it. Today is Tue 13 Oct; Mon 12 has last week's weekly digests; Thu 8 has a digest that failed to write. Day texts other than today's are samples.

Phone. Edge swipe on Digest, Topics or Papers opens the drawer. On anything deeper it drags the page back with the previous one underneath; let go past ~40% or flick to go back. On a phone, Android's own back gesture walks the same steps.

Questions

  1. Which way through the levels: A pages, B one unfolding column, C colour grid with a slide-over? Or a mix, such as C's grid above B's column?
  2. The grid in C: topics as rows in their fixed order, days as columns, each cell the colour of that digest's best paper. Does it work as History for you?
  3. On a day, each digest shows its Gist and up to 3 pick titles. Right amount, or should picks show their Gists too, or only the Gist with no titles?
  4. This week's weekly digests: a pinned group at the top of the Digest tab (B), only in History (A), or a W column in the grid (C)?
  5. "Nothing new" digests keep their slot. Full block as now, or a thin one-line row in the same slot?

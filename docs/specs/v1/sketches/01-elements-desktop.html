litwatch v1 · element sheet · desktop

The building blocks every screen is made of, each in light and dark. One type system (Inter, tabular figures; JetBrains Mono for arXiv ids), one look: a dense, neutral tool surface where the only strong colour is signal, rarity on the left edge of every row and topic badges on the right. Nothing on a row ever moves.

Palette for the whole sheet:

0 · Type and look

Everything is one sans at a few sizes. No serif anywhere, including the Deep card. Rows sit on a surface one step lighter (light) or darker (dark) than the page, separated by hairlines, not cards.

1 · Rarity scale

Five levels as loot colours, on the left edge of every row, with the rank glyph (four bars, one more filled per level) as the non-colour backup. Legendary rows also get a faint tint of their colour so they read as a different row, not only a different edge. Three palettes, each in light and dark; the button at the top switches the whole sheet so the lists further down can be judged in each.

A classic is the loot convention (grey, green, blue, purple, orange). B calm keeps the same hue order at lower saturation, tuned for a list read every morning. C sparse drops the stripe on common and greys uncommon, so in a long mixed list only rare and above carry colour at all. The rank glyph is identical in all three.

2 · Topic badges

One icon and one hue per followed topic, like T3 Code's project icons. Depth is the box, not the colour: Focus solid, Area tinted, Field outlined. Row size 18 px (phone 20), header size 28 px. The hue is a different layer from rarity: badges sit at the right end of a row in a box, stripes at the left edge, so the two never share a position.

3 · A paper at each detail level

Every row has the same slots left to right: stripe, rank glyph, title, badges, saved mark. A Gist row adds two lines under the title and nothing else moves. On a topic page the badge slot is dropped, since every row is the same topic.

4 · Paper page pieces

Header, then the ladder downwards: Brief (or the abstract in its place), the Deep card, then the paper's neighbours. The Deep card is the same type as everything else, with small bold headings; prior work and "among the collection" are the same row shape, the whole row a link to the paper page.

5 · Digest pieces

History is days, newest first, each day the topics' Marks in one fixed order (Focus, Area, Field); a topic with nothing new keeps its slot. The stripe is the best paper's colour. A day opens to each topic's Gist with up to three pick Marks.

6 · Controls

Save is a bookmark that fills. Thumbs are quiet until used; a thumb opens its reasons as chips and the acknowledgement says what happened. Filters are a toolbar on a desktop and one button opening a sheet on the phone. The status line is one dot and a sentence, in the header beside the wordmark. The gear is the only way into Settings.

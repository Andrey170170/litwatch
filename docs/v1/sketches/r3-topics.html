Round 3 · topic page and Papers

Rows All rounds

A topic page, the Papers tab and the Topics tab, on the chosen shell. All three tabs share one set of controls: a rarity bar (five segments, sized by count; tap one or more to filter), Saved, Code, a search box and the sort order. On the phone these sit in the bottom bar and in a sheet that bar opens. The rows come in three forms: A a dense table, one line per paper; B a reading list of title and Gist, grouped by week; C shelves by rarity, with the top shelves open and the rest folded to a count. A topic page also shows its recent digests as a strip of squares. Papers searches the collection and, once you type, the archive, whose papers sit in their own band with Process. This page opens on Interpretability; the sidebar and drawer reach the other topics. The ★ on a row saves the paper; the Digest tab here is a placeholder.

Questions

  1. Rows: A, B or C? They can differ by place: for example C on a topic page, A in Papers, B on the phone.
  2. The rarity bar as the main filter: right? On a topic page its levels read as grades (hover a segment); in Papers they are the mixed rarity.
  3. Papers sorts newest first, or by best match once you type. Should a topic page sort newest first too, or rarest first?
  4. Phone: search and filters in the bottom bar, the filters in a sheet. Good, or should the rarity bar stay visible at the top of the list?
  5. The Topics tab: cards with each topic's recent digests as squares. Enough, or should it show more, such as counts per rarity or the newest must read?

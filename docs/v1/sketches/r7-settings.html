What changed in 7b

  • Overview holds how the pipeline is doing: four tiles (what needs the owner, last night, this month's spend, the last Calibrate session), then recall, precision, reactions and matters with no topic. Needs you holds only what waits on the owner.
  • On the paper's page, the same items: a note under the head in the same colour (yellow asks for a word, red reports a failure) with the same actions, and the topic row in the rail marked (a split keeps its Fit control open; ungraded shows red). On the phone the note's Label opens the ⋯ sheet, where the topic is marked too. Settling it on either page settles it on both.
  • Calibrate: the strip of squares jumps; on the phone the number in the bar opens a list of the session's papers. Past sessions open a review: the owner's Fit against the pipeline's, amber where they differ.
  • The re-run offer takes the place of Save and Cancel, in the topic editor and in the class editor. Not now leaves it in Runs and cost › Jobs as "not run", to run now, with tonight's run, or discard.
  • Jobs and an integration's events are pages of their own. Topics drag by ⋮⋮; Taxonomy's carets fold, the row opens the editor.

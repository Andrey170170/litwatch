What is here

  • Eight pages in three groups: Reading (Quality, Calibrate, Misses), Following (Topics, Taxonomy), System (Runs and cost, Integrations, Configuration). Two desktop layouts, one switch.
  • Quality: "Needs you" first, grouped by kind. Yellow edge asks for the owner's word (splits and escalations, settled with the Fit control); red reports a failure, with its actions. A settled item turns cyan and stays until the next visit. Then "How it is doing": recall, precision, reactions, matters with no topic, cost.
  • Calibrate: one paper per screen, blind (no grade, rarity or topic marks), the questions as segmented controls, a strip of 20 squares for the session.
  • Topic editor: a page from Topics; Save puts the scoped re-run offer at the top, in the note's style, with counts, costs and a ceiling. The class editor on Taxonomy opens in its row and offers the same.
  • Misses: each report with its trace as five stage cells, red where the paper was let go.

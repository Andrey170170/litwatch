litwatch v1 · UI sketches

From general to fine. Each round asks a few questions with variants side by side; the answers fix that level before the next one starts. Phone pages are meant to be opened on the phone.

  1. Shell: where navigation lives, how screens divide space. desktop · phone. Chosen: desktop B (sidebar), phone C (drawer, opened by an edge swipe).
  2. 1b · Direction: what it feels like. Neat, Night City, Newsprint, Mission control on the chosen shell, with the swipe drawer. open. Chosen: Night City, toned down (no scanlines, plainer numbers), dark first.
  3. Digest: History, a day, a digest; how far each level opens in place. A pages · B unfolding column · C colour grid. Pooled into 2b: Day · Week · History (list or calendar), digests and papers sliding over. Slimmed in 2c: whole-card clicks, a month calendar, three phone control layouts, the run status in the wordmark. Then 2d: Month calendar chosen, History loading back to the archive's start, P4 (views in the drawer, controls at the bottom) against P1. Chosen: P4, Month calendar, swipe across a Day or Week to step it.
  4. Topic page and Papers: rows, filters, sorting, search. open: A table · B reading list · C shelves, one switch; rarity bar as filter; phone search and filters at the bottom. Chosen: C shelves, Newest or Rarest inside each shelf. 3b chosen: rarity bar inline with the controls, rich Topics cards (one-line titles, capped digest squares).
  5. Paper page: the ladder (Gist, Brief, Deep card, the paper), topics with thumbs and labels, Save and links. open: A one column · B with a rail · C folded; slide-over or page on desktop; five paper states; phone swipe through the list. Chosen: B as a page. Then 4b: the rail as what stays at hand (Gist, Save, BibTeX, thumbs and labels), names in the side column, the phone's ⋯ sheet. Then 4c: the Gist moves from the column to the rail as it scrolls away; actions as a rail grid or in the header; the sheet with one Fit control per topic. Chosen: the rail as Gist, topics, then the action grid.
  6. Look: the whole app with the look as switches. open: UI face, reading face, text size, topic badge colour, rarity glow, Night City detail; Light and Dark. Chosen: Chakra Petch throughout, text as now, coloured badges, Night City detail as now, the glow at 20% over 220px on Digest rows, about the same length at three quarters of the strength on topic pages and Papers, 0.6 of the length on the paper header. 5c: phone digest counts on their own line.
  7. Interactions: how it responds, as switches on the chosen look. open: reason first or thumb first, a toast or a note by the control, desktop row hover, page motion (none, subtle, Night City), phone sheets dragged down to close; press feedback throughout. Chosen: reason first, the brighter glow on hover, sheets dragged down to close. 6b: the toast in the note's style, only for results the page does not show; Night City motion as the subtle slide with a title glitch. Chosen: the toast as in 6b, page motion subtle.
  8. Settings: the pages behind the gear on the chosen app. open: Quality (what needs the owner, then how the pipeline is doing), Calibrate, Misses, Topics and the topic editor with the re-run offer, Taxonomy, Runs and cost, Integrations, Configuration; Settings in the sidebar or as an index in the page; numbers or bars. Chosen: Settings takes the sidebar, bars. 7b: Overview (how the pipeline is doing) apart from Needs you (only what waits on the owner); the same open states on the paper's page; Calibrate's strip jumps and past sessions open; Jobs and an integration's events as pages; the re-run offer in place of Save, Not now leaving it in Jobs.

Reference: Fable's first element sheet, desktop · phone (parked for round 5).

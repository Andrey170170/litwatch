The screens chosen so far, in one app, with the look as switches. Each switch changes one thing; the first option of each is the look as it stands. They apply to the desktop and the phone together, and work in Light as well as Dark. Jump to:

The switches

  • UI face: Chakra Petch (now) or IBM Plex Sans for everything that is not a heading. Headings and caps stay Rajdhani, numbers stay mono.
  • Reading face: the written text (Gists, digest takes, the Brief, the Deep card's prose) in the UI face, or in Newsreader, a serif made for screen reading.
  • Text: as now, or one step (10%) larger with the same spacing.
  • Topic badges: coloured everywhere (now); coloured only where the topic is the subject (the sidebar, topic and digest heads, the paper's topic rows) and grey on paper rows, where colour means rarity; or grey everywhere. Filled, tinted and outlined still tell Focus, Area and Field apart.
  • Rarity on rows: the stripe (now), or the stripe plus a glow of its colour fading across the row.
  • Night City detail: as now (cut corners, red section names, //), or plainer.

Questions

  1. Type: which UI face, and which reading face for the Brief and the Deep card?
  2. Text size: as now or a step up, on the desktop and on the phone?
  3. Topic badges: coloured everywhere, only where the topic is the subject, or grey?
  4. Rarity on rows: the stripe alone, or with the glow?
  5. Night City detail: as now, or plainer?
